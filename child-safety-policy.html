Bizning majburiyatimiz

Our Commitment

Sovchilar.net — foydalanuvchilar o'rtasida nikoh maqsadida aloqa o'rnatishga yordam beradigan platforma. Biz barcha foydalanuvchilar, xususan voyaga yetmaganlarning xavfsizligini ta'minlashga qat'iy majburmiz.

Platformamiz faqat 18 yoshdan katta shaxslar uchun mo'ljallangan. Bolalarga zarar yetkazuvchi har qanday kontent yoki xatti-harakat qat'iyan taqiqlanadi va darhol tegishli organlarga xabar qilinadi.

Sovchilar.net is a matchmaking platform that helps users connect for the purpose of marriage. We are firmly committed to the safety of all users, especially minors.

Our platform is intended solely for individuals 18 years of age or older. Any content or behavior that harms children is strictly prohibited and will be immediately reported to the relevant authorities.

🚫

Taqiqlangan xatti-harakatlar (CSAE)

Prohibited Conduct (CSAE)

Quyidagilar platformada mutlaqo taqiqlanadi:

  • Bolalarga nisbatan jinsiy tazyiq, zo'ravonlik yoki ekspluatatsiya
  • Voyaga yetmaganlarni o'z ichiga olgan har qanday kontent yaratish yoki tarqatish
  • Bolalarni tanishuvga yoki zararli munosabatlarga jalb qilish
  • Voyaga yetmagan shaxs sifatida ro'yxatdan o'tish
  • Bolalarning shaxsiy ma'lumotlarini to'plash yoki tarqatish
Ushbu qoidalarni buzish hisobni darhol bloklash va huquq-tartibot idoralariga murojaat qilishga olib keladi.

The following are strictly prohibited on our platform:

  • Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form
  • Creating, distributing, or storing any content involving minors
  • Grooming or soliciting minors for any relationship
  • Registering or creating a profile as or for a minor
  • Collecting or sharing personal data of children
Violations of these rules result in immediate account suspension and referral to law enforcement authorities.
✅

Xavfsizlik choralari

Safety Measures

  • Ro'yxatdan o'tishda 18 yoshdan katta ekanlik tasdiqlash majburiy
  • Shubhali xatti-harakatlar avtomatik tizim orqali aniqlanadi
  • Foydalanuvchilar boshqalarni ilova ichidan xabar qilishi mumkin
  • Barcha shikoyatlar xavfsizlik jamoasi tomonidan ko'rib chiqiladi
  • Qoidabuzarlik holatlari davlat organlariga uzatiladi
  • Age verification (18+) is required at registration
  • Suspicious behavior is detected through automated systems
  • Users can report others directly from within the app
  • All reports are reviewed by our safety team
  • Violations are escalated to the relevant authorities
