Child Safety Policy

Sovchilar.net – Matrimonial App

CSAE ZERO TOLERANCE

This policy is published in English for global accessibility. Uzbek and Russian versions are available below each section.

1. Purpose and Scope

Sovchilar.net is a matrimonial platform designed exclusively for adults seeking lawful marriage partnerships. This Child Safety Policy applies to all users, administrators, and third parties who interact with the platform.

UZ: Sovchilar.net — faqat kattalar uchun mo'ljallangan nikoh platformasi. Ushbu siyosat platformaning barcha foydalanuvchilari va uchinchi tomonlarga tatbiq etiladi.

2. Zero Tolerance for Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Sovchilar.net strictly prohibits any content, behavior, or activity that constitutes Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) or Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

The following are absolutely prohibited on the platform:

  • Uploading, sharing, or distributing any sexually explicit material involving minors.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or attempting to establish sexual contact with a minor.
  • Using the platform to traffic, exploit, or harm children in any way.
  • Creating or sharing content that sexualizes, objectifies, or endangers minors.
  • Attempting to circumvent age verification to interact with minors.

Any user found to have violated these prohibitions will be permanently banned and their information will be reported to the relevant law enforcement authorities in accordance with applicable law.

UZ: Sovchilar.net platformasida bolalarga nisbatan har qanday jinsiy zo'ravonlik, ekspluatatsiya va zararli kontent qat'iyan taqiqlanadi. Buzuvchilar doimiy ravishda bloklanadi va huquq-tartibot organlariga topshiriladi.

3. Age Restriction

Sovchilar.net is strictly limited to users aged 18 years and older. Minors are not permitted to register or use the platform under any circumstances. Age verification steps are applied during registration, and accounts suspected of being operated by minors are subject to immediate suspension pending review.

UZ: Platformadan foydalanish faqat 18 yoshdan kattalar uchun ruxsat etiladi. Voyaga yetmaganlar ro'yxatdan o'ta olmaydi. Shubhali hisoblar darhol tekshiriladi va to'xtatiladi.

If you encounter any content or behavior that you believe violates this policy or endangers a child, please report it immediately using one of the following methods:

  • In-app: Open the app → Profile → Menu → "Bolalar xavfsizligi" → Submit report.
  • Email: shahzodburhonov7@gmail.com

All reports are reviewed within 24 hours. Reports involving imminent danger to a child will be escalated immediately to law enforcement.

5. Enforcement and Consequences

Violations of this Child Safety Policy result in the following actions:

  • Immediate and permanent account suspension.
  • Preservation of evidence and reporting to national and international law enforcement agencies.
  • Cooperation with NCMEC (National Center for Missing & Exploited Children) CyberTipline and equivalent bodies.
  • Legal action where applicable.
